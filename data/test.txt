Q: How do you protect yourself from phishing?
A: You stay alert and verify everything. Check the sender’s email address carefully. Do not click suspicious links or download unknown attachments. Look for signs like urgent language or fake offers. Use strong passwords and enable two factor authentication. When in doubt, go directly to the official website instead of using links in messages.

You need to be stricter than this in real life. Most people fail because they trust too fast. Train yourself to slow down before every click.
